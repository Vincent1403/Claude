Fragment Motion

The five gradient-fragment visuals from the Figma page, rebuilt as live geometry. Each fragment still carries the whole gradient, fitted to its own bounding box. The motion comes from moving the folds and turning the light behind them.

Format

Move the pointer across the stage to steer the light yourself.

Fit

Every fragment is filled with the full gradient, stretched to its own bounding box. That is how the vectors are filled in the Figma file, and the rule is recomputed on every frame.

Fold

The geometry travels. The disc's rings grow outward by a factor of √2 and loop without a seam, rays sweep around their focus, chevron bands slide down the weave. As a fragment changes shape, its gradient re-fits.

Light

The box each gradient is fitted to turns slowly, as if the lamp behind the membrane were moving, and a soft pulse runs across the fragments.
