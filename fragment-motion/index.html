Fit

Every fragment is filled with the full gradient, stretched to its own bounding box, as in the Figma file. The Gradient settings turn and slide that fit; the pointer does nothing.

Fold

The structure folds shut and open once every five seconds at speed 1.0, so one clip holds one full fold. Pleats turn edge-on, fans gather to one side, lenses close to slits, scallops flatten.

Build

Piece by piece adds the fragments one after another, each growing out of the one before. Unfold starts tightly folded and opens the whole structure in one move. With a build selected, exported clips start with it.

Round

Lens rosette, Scallop drum, Fluted rows and Swirl iris take the almond openings, the scalloped edge, the rounded pleat ends and the curved pleats from the lamp's lower ring and treat them with the same gradient rule.
